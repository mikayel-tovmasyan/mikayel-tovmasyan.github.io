Teaching

Teaching is a central part of my academic work. I bring extensive experience in economics instruction, from introductory courses to graduate seminars and thesis supervision.

My experience includes independent teaching at the Catholic University of Eichstätt-Ingolstadt, Technische Hochschule Ingolstadt, the American University of Armenia, and ICARE, as well as tutorials at KU and the University of Bayreuth.

I teach in English and German, with experience across microeconomics, macroeconomics, public economics, development economics, and quantitative methods.
